The specialist council

Memory, performance and correctness specialists share one observation. Numerical and feasibility vetoes beat majority voting. The controller changes only our own workload at supported step boundaries.

Runtime replay combines real GPU training with a declared logical budget trace and bounded same-process memory reservation. It is not a gaming experiment or a multi-application SLO claim.

Evidence and limits

These results concern one laptop GPU and a small repeated graph processor. They do not establish production NVIDIA benefits, performance on Hopper/Grace systems, novel GPU hardware, or a universal offload policy. PhysicsNeMo already combines graph optimizations with checkpointing and offloading.

Implementation: C++/CUDA dual-CSR sum and transpose backward; PyTorch/PyG/sparse-library references; dense activation offloading; checkpointing with RNG preservation; validated profile registry; bounded controller and hash-chained event ledger.
